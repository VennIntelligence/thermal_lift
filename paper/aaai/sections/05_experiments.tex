\subsection{Frame-Budget Analysis}

A classical CPU subset matrix is evaluated for drizzle and TGV with
$N\in\{31,62,124,248\}$ using phase-stratified sampling. Drizzle shows a clear
information-budget trend as $N$ increases (better split consistency, lower
artifact, and higher FRC), whereas TGV keeps lower artifact but exhibits
non-monotonic raw-control correlation.

\subsection{Robustness}

Shift perturbation stress tests add Gaussian noise
$\sigma\in\{0,0.05,0.1,0.2\}$ pixels to contour-refined shifts. Raw-control
correlation is relatively stable for both drizzle and TGV under this small
noise range, while coverage/FRC proxies degrade more clearly. Replacing
command-prior shifts with contour-refined shifts yields large end-to-end gains,
confirming that data-driven alignment refinement is effective; command shifts
remain priors, not truth.

\subsection{Checkpoint Selection Protocol in Practice}

Per-variant Pareto scatter is built on (artifact, corr), with a fixed TGV
reference point. Canonical checkpoints are selected jointly from metric trend
and visual panels, then contrasted against 60K endpoints. Endpoint-only
reporting would systematically present the worst drifted model for each
variant.

\subsection{Negative Results}

The PixelShuffle HR-head control performs worse than the bilinear head in this
setting and introduces stripe-like artifacts. We keep it only as an attribution
control. Networks targeting $4\times$ output do not show credible gains under
the measured MTF constraints, and rendered AVI bursts are excluded as SR inputs
because of 8-bit quantization and heavy frame duplication.

\subsection{Held-Out ROI-2 Visual/Proxy Audit}

To verify that the visual gate is not a single-ROI coincidence, we repeat
structure proxies on an independent second ROI (upper-right plate
edge/zigzag-branch region). Lattice ranking remains consistent with the center
ROI (drizzle $<$ TGV $<$ Hybrid+ResObs $<$ Hybrid-60K), while sharpness and
zigzag-profile ordering is not fully stable across ROIs. We therefore report
ROI-2 as a held-out audit, not as winner certification.
