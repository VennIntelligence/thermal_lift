\section{Discussion and Limitations}

The experiments support a bounded claim: the main session contains enough
micro-scan information to build a 2x contour-level POC, but the conclusion is
sensitive to alignment quality and forward-model residual interpretation.

Three negative results are central to the current paper draft. First, filename
or stage-command alignment is not accurate enough to serve as truth. Second,
tuned contour alignment improves EP05 Chamfer but does not automatically
improve EP06 SR stability. Third, MAP-TV is not the best or sharpest method in
the current data-driven sweep; it is a conservative candidate that suppresses
unstable high-frequency structure.

Future work should add MTF/edge-transfer validation, cross-ROI stability, and a
customer-facing evaluation set. The final report should continue to separate
detector pitch, calibrated spatial resolution, and output-grid sampling.
